Hosting should be the fun part.

Hosting should feel good, but planning takes time and skills many of us never had. Whether you are a student short on space or a parent short on time, party stores sell supplies by the case and every theme means another trip. Our purpose is simple: give you everything for a great party in one box, so all you have to do is show up to your own party.

No more store hopping

Plates, decor, and extras come matched in one box, so you skip the five stores and the endless scrolling.

No design skills needed

Every box has a host card with a timeline, a playlist QR code, drink and snack ideas, and a cleanup checklist.

Easy on your walls

Every box includes damage-free hooks, removable tape, and strips, so nothing gets damaged in a dorm, a rental, or your own home.

What is in every box.

Every box is themed and matched, serves 8 guests, and works without nails, tape residue, or a trip to the store.
