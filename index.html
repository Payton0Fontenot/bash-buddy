Hosting should be the fun part.

College students love to host, but planning takes time and skills most of us never had. Space is tight, the rules are strict, and the store sells party supplies by the case. Our purpose is simple: give you everything for a great night in one box, so all you have to do is show up to your own party.

No more store hopping

Plates, decor, and extras come matched in one box, so you skip the five stores and the endless scrolling.

No design skills needed

Every box has a host card with a timeline, a playlist QR code, drink and snack ideas, and a cleanup checklist.

Safe for your deposit

Dorm and lease rules limit nails and mess, so every box includes damage-free hooks, removable tape, and strips.
