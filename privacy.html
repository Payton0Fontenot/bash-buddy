Policies

Privacy

What we collect and how we use it. Last updated October 27, 2026.

What we collect

When you reserve a Birthday Bash box, we collect your first name, email address, party date, approximate guest count, and the price shown to you on our site. When you join our list, we collect your email address and any themes or hosting details you choose to share. When orders open, your name and shipping address will be collected at checkout, and your payment details are handled directly by Stripe, so we never receive or store your full card number.

How we use it

We use your information to save your spot, send you first access and your 5% first-order offer, share Bash Buddy Co. news like launches and new themes, and fulfill orders once they open. We also use reservation details, such as party dates and the prices people saw, to understand interest and plan our launch.

Site analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page visits, such as how many people view a page. It reports totals, does not use cookies to track you, and does not tell us who you are.

Who we share it with

We never sell your information. We share it only with the services that help us run Bash Buddy Co.: Google Forms and Google Sheets, which store reservations; Vercel, which hosts our site and provides analytics; and, once orders open, Stripe for payments and shipping carriers to deliver your box. Each handles your information under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you're on our list or have an open order, and for as long as needed for business records. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from our emails at any time, or ask us for a copy of your information, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing bashbuddyco@gmail.com.

Children

Our site is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy as Bash Buddy Co. grows. The date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email bashbuddyco@gmail.com or use our contact page.
