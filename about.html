About Bash Buddy Co.

I loved to host.
I hated the hassle.

Bash Buddy Co. started as a class project and this first box is just the beginning. Here's how it started.

PaytonFounder

It wasn't just me. People love hosting, but hate the process.

In college, every party I threw turned into a hassle. I drove all over town for plates, decorations, and supplies, and as a student, I just did not have that kind of time.

The more I talked to other hosts, the more I realized I wasn't the only one. That's where the idea for a whole party in one box began.

The idea

So I put the whole party in one box.

Decor, a party activity, a host guide, and a cleanup kit, all in one theme and shipped to your door.
