About Bash Buddy Co.

Throw the party. Skip the stress.

Hosting should be the fun part.

Bash Buddy Co. makes themed party boxes for people who want hosting to be easy again, from college students in dorms and apartments to busy parents at home. Each box has a photo op, a party activity, a cleanup kit, and a host guide, so you can host without a shopping trip or design skills.
