Meet the founder

Hi, I'm Payton.

In college, I loved to host. But every party turned into a hassle, driving all over town for plates, decorations, and supplies, and as a student, I just did not have that kind of time.

It wasn't just me. People love hosting, but hate the process.

That is why I started Bash Buddy Co. Every box has everything you need for a cohesive themed party, from decor to cleanup, so you can skip the errands and spend the night with your guests. We are starting right here in Grand Junction with the Birthday Bash, and more themes are on the way.
