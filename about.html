Meet the founder

Hi, I'm Payton.

What started as a class project has become a lifelong passion. In college, I loved to host. But every party turned into a hassle, driving all over town for plates, decorations, and supplies, and as a student, I just did not have that kind of time.

I finally realized it was not just me. So many people love to host but struggle to pull it off.

That is why I started Bash Buddy Co. Every box has everything you need for a cohesive themed party, from decor to cleanup, so you can skip the errands and spend the night with your guests. We are starting right here in Grand Junction with the Birthday Bash, and more themes are on the way.
