About Bash Buddy Co.

Throw the party. Skip the stress.

Hosting should be the fun part.

Bash Buddy Co. makes themed party boxes for people who want hosting to be easy again, from college students in dorms and apartments to busy parents at home. Each box has the decor, tableware, a game or craft, and a host card, so you can host without a shopping trip or design skills.

Made for small spaces

Every box serves 8 guests and includes a table setting, decor, a backdrop, a game or craft, a host card, an invite template, and the damage-free hanging kit.
