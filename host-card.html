Included in every box

The host card.

A simple plan for your party, from the first invite to the last trash bag.

Every Bash Buddy box comes with a host card made for its theme. It tells you what to do and when, so you can stop planning and start hosting.

Shop the Birthday BashSee a sample

What is on it

01

A party timeline

What to do the week before, the hour before, and once guests arrive, so nothing sneaks up on you.

02

A playlist QR code

Scan it to start a playlist that matches the theme. No scrolling for songs mid-party.

03

Drink and snack ideas

Easy menu ideas that fit the theme. Food and drinks are not included, so you can shop or order what you like.

04

A cleanup checklist

A step-by-step list for taking everything down without a mark, so the night ends easy.

Sample

A Birthday Bash, start to finish.

Here is the kind of timeline you will find on the Birthday Bash host card. Every theme gets its own.

  1. A week before
    Send the invite

    Customize the invite template and text it to your guests.

  2. An hour before
    Set the scene

    Hang the backdrop and banner with the damage-free kit, build the balloon garland, and set the table.

  3. Guests arrive
    Start the music

    Scan the playlist QR code and put out the snack board.

  4. Mid-party
    Play the game

    Break out the birthday trivia cards about the guest of honor.

  5. The big moment
    Cake and photos

    Cut the cupcakes under the backdrop for the group photo.

  6. After
    Clean up

    Run the cleanup checklist and peel everything off without a mark.
