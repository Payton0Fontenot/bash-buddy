The host guide.

A simple plan for your party, from the first invite to the last trash bag.

Every Bash Buddy box comes with a host guide made for its theme. It tells you what to do and when, so you can stop planning and start hosting.

What is on it

01

A party timeline

What to do the week before, the hour before, and once guests arrive, so nothing sneaks up on you.

02

A playlist QR code

Scan it to start a playlist that matches the theme. No scrolling for songs mid-party.

03

A food and portion guide

Easy menu ideas that fit the theme, with simple amounts for your guest count so you don't overbuy. Food and drinks are not included.

04

A small-space setup guide

Where the backdrop, banner, and seating go, so the party fits a dorm, an apartment, or a living room.

05

A 10-minute takedown checklist

A quick plan for peeling everything off without a mark and packing reusable decor back in the bag, so the decor never stays up for days.

Sample

A Birthday Bash, start to finish.

Here is the kind of timeline you will find on the Birthday Bash host guide. Every theme gets its own.

  1. A week before
    Send the invite

    Customize the invite template and text it to your guests.

  2. An hour before
    Set the scene

    Hang the backdrop and banner with the damage-free strips, using the small-space setup guide.

  3. Guests arrive
    Start the music

    Scan the playlist QR code and put out the snack board, sized with the portion guide.

  4. Mid-party
    Play the game

    Break out the party game (TBD).

  5. The big moment
    Cake and photos

    Cut the cupcakes under the backdrop for the group photo.

  6. After
    Clean up

    Run the 10-minute takedown checklist, peel everything off without a mark, and pack reusable decor back in the bag.
