What comes in a box?

Every box is themed and includes:

  • Photo op: a themed backdrop and banner for the group shot
  • Cleanup kit: damage-free strips, a pack-it-back bag, trash bags, and wipes
  • Activity: a built-in party activity
  • Host guide: a timeline, food and portion guide, small-space setup guide, 10-minute takedown checklist, and playlist QR code
  • Invitation template: a themed invite to text or post

Our first box is the Birthday Bash. See the Birthday Bash page for everything inside.

Will you have other themes?

Yes. The Birthday Bash is our first box, and more themes are on the way. To get first dibs and tell us which ones you want next, Join The Party!

How many guests does a box serve?

Any size group. Nothing in the box limits your guest count, and the food and portion guide in your host guide helps you plan for however many people you invite.

Will it work in my dorm, apartment, or home?

Boxes are designed for small spaces, with a small-space setup guide and damage-free strips that peel off clean. Housing rules differ, so check your own rules about candles, balloons, and adhesives.

Can I customize my party?

Yes. Every box has a customizable invitation template you can text or post, so the invite matches your party.

How does delivery work?

Your box ships right to your door. Shipping is added at checkout. See Shipping and returns for details.

How far ahead should I order?

Boxes launch in 2027, and we'll share exact packing and delivery times when orders open. Since delivery times are estimates, we recommend ordering well before your party.

Can I return something?

Yes. Unused, unopened boxes can be returned within 14 days of delivery for a refund of the box price, minus shipping. Opened or used boxes, and custom or personalized items, can't be returned. See Shipping and returns for details.
