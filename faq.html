What comes in a box?

Each box is themed and includes a table setting, decor like a balloon garland and banner, a backdrop, a game or craft, a host card, an invitation template, and a damage-free hanging kit. Exact contents vary by theme, so check the theme page.

How many guests does a box serve?

About 8 guests.

Will it work in my dorm, apartment, or home?

Boxes are designed for small spaces and include damage-free hooks, removable tape, and strips. Housing rules differ, so check your own rules about candles, balloons, and adhesives.

Do you sell alcohol?

No. Alcohol is never included. Drink kits contain recipe cards, labels, and supplies only, and cocktail items are for guests 21 and older.

Can I customize my party?

Yes. Every box has a customizable invitation template, and you can add a custom banner with your name or message.
